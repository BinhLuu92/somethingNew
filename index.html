    Our story

    Butterbell lives in an old bell-maker's workshop. The ground floor is the café and the oven; upstairs there are six seats by the window and a shelf of books you can borrow.

    Our sourdough starter is called Dot and she is older than the café. The beans come from a roaster two streets away and are roasted every Tuesday. Strawberries come from the Hale family farm from May to September, and the cake changes when they stop.
